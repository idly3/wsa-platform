Accounting · Q14Asked at 9 firms

Depreciation goes up by $10. Walk me through the three statements.

Operating income falls $10; at a 25% tax rate net income falls $7.50. On the cash flow statement you add back the $10, so cash rises $2.50. PP&E is down $10 and cash up $2.50, so assets fall $7.50, matched by retained earnings.
