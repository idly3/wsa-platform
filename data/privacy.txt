This Privacy Policy explains how Wall Street Alpha, LLC ("Wall Street Alpha," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit

By using the Site or Services, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site or Services.
1. Information We Collect
Information you give us directly
When you sign up for our newsletter, contact us, book a coaching session, or purchase a program, you may provide information such as your name, email address, and any details you choose to share about your background or goals.
Payment information
When you make a purchase, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our own servers. Stripe collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy, available at 
https://stripe.com/privacy. We receive limited information from Stripe such as confirmation of payment, the last four digits of your card, and billing details.
Information collected automatically
When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. This may be collected through cookies and similar technologies.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
Provide, operate, and deliver our coaching and mentorship Services
Process payments and manage your account
Send you the newsletter and other communications you have requested
Respond to your questions and provide customer support
Understand how the Site is used and improve it
Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these situations:
