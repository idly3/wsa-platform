Questions from the lecture

What students and parents ask.

Watch the Q&A · 1:13:05
I'm a freshman. Is it too early?

It's the best time to start. Freshmen have the easiest path of anyone: no bad habits to unlearn, and the whole recruiting calendar in front of them. Watch my answer · 1:13:11

I go to a target school. Do I still need this?

Your competition isn't the student at a non-target. It's the person next to you in the same club, applying to the same seats. Watch my answer · 1:13:22

I'm an international student. Can you help?

Yes, as long as you're studying in, or a citizen of, the US or the country you're applying in. A lot of our coaches were international students themselves. Watch my answer · 1:13:32

Can you guarantee an offer?

No one can honestly promise an offer, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What we do guarantee is that we stay by your side: you keep the platform, the question banks and text access to your coach until you land an offer, however many cycles that takes. Watch my answer · 52:43

What's your placement rate?

It's strong, but we don't publish it. A lot of programs put a big placement rate on their website. When we stopped leading with ours, our results got better: students stopped coming to us expecting a miracle and started coming ready to do the work. The students who land offers aren't the ones with the best school or GPA. They're the ones who send the emails and do the mock interviews every week, with a coach in their corner. If you're looking for a shortcut, the Academy isn't for you. Watch my answer · 1:13:51

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We'll go through the options on your strategy call. Watch my answer · 1:13:46

Why a call instead of a checkout page?

Every student's year and timeline are different, and cohorts are capped. On the call we look at your resume, target banks and timeline, map a plan, and tell you honestly whether the Academy is the right fit. If it isn't, we'll point you in the right direction. Watch my answer · 1:09:06
