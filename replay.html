Before you book

How to make your application count.

  • Meet the GPA minimum for your year. 3.0 for freshmen, 3.1 for sophomores, 3.3 for juniors and up.
  • Have your resume and LinkedIn ready. You'll upload your resume after you book, and we read it before the call.
  • Come prepared. Know the roles you want and your timeline, and be ready to start.
  • Bring every decision maker. Anyone helping you decide or pay joins the call.

Questions from the lecture

What students and parents ask.

I'm a freshman. Is it too early?

It's the best time to start. Freshmen have the easiest path of anyone: no bad habits to unlearn, and the whole recruiting calendar in front of them.

I go to a target school. Do I still need this?

Your competition isn't the student at a non-target. It's the person next to you in the same club, applying to the same seats.

I'm an international student. Can you help?

Yes, as long as you're studying in, or a citizen of, the US or the country you're applying in. A lot of our coaches were international students themselves.

Can you guarantee an offer?

No one can honestly promise an offer, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What we do guarantee is that we stay by your side: you keep the platform, the question banks and text access to your coach until you land an offer, however many cycles that takes.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We'll go through the options on your strategy call.

Why a call instead of a checkout page?

Every student's year and timeline are different, and cohorts are capped at 12. On the call we look at your resume, target banks and timeline, map a plan, and tell you honestly whether the Academy is the right fit. If it isn't, we'll point you in the right direction.
